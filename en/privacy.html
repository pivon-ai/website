Privacy policy

This is a courtesy translation. Only the German version is legally binding: Datenschutzerklärung.

1. Overview

This website is deliberately lean. It sets no cookies, embeds no third-party services and uses no analytics or tracking tools. When you open a page, only files from the server hosting this website are loaded (see section 3) — no request goes to any other provider.

We therefore process personal data in only two cases: in the server log files that are technically generated when a page is requested, and when you choose to write to us or call us.

The controller is the natural or legal person who, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by:

Vercel Inc.
440 N Barranca Ave #4133
Covina, CA 91723, USA

Vercel delivers the pages via a worldwide network of servers; your request is usually answered by a server near you. On our behalf, the provider processes the data that is technically generated when the website is accessed. For this purpose, we have concluded a data processing agreement with the provider pursuant to Article 28 GDPR (Vercel’s Data Processing Addendum).

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. Vercel Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is therefore covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission pursuant to Article 45 GDPR. In addition, the data processing agreement contains the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses pursuant to Article 46 GDPR.

When a page is requested, the server automatically stores information in so-called log files. Which information this is and how long it is kept is determined by the hosting provider. Typically, this includes:

  • the address requested and the time of the request
  • the amount of data transferred and whether the request succeeded
  • browser type and version as well as the operating system used
  • the previously visited page, if your browser transmits it
  • IP address

We do not combine this data with other sources and do not analyse it on a personal basis. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the technically error-free operation and security of the website.

The hosting provider stores the log files for one hour.

4. Enquiries by email, phone or post

If you write to us or call us, we store your enquiry together with the personal data it contains in order to process it and in case you have follow-up questions. We do not pass on this data without your consent.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to the performance of a contract or serves to prepare a contract. In all other cases, the processing is based on our legitimate interest in handling enquiries addressed to us pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR or on your consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, if you have given it.

Your enquiry remains with us until you ask us to delete it, withdraw your consent or the purpose of storage no longer applies. Mandatory statutory retention periods remain unaffected.

5. No cookies, no analytics, no third-party services

We have measured the following points on the finished version of this website:

  • No cookies. The website sets none, neither its own nor third-party ones.
  • No browser storage. Neither local storage nor session storage nor a browser database is written to.
  • No external connections. When you open a page, all requests go to our hosting provider’s server. There are no embedded maps, videos, social media buttons or font services.
  • Fonts are hosted with the site. The fonts used are delivered together with the website. Your IP address is not transmitted to any font provider.
  • No audience measurement. We use no analytics tools and create no usage profiles.

This is why this website does not need a consent banner. Should anything change, we will update this policy first.

6. Encrypted transmission

This website transmits its content in encrypted form via HTTPS. You can recognise this by the padlock symbol in your browser’s address bar. With encryption active, the data you transmit to us cannot be read by third parties.

7. Your rights

You have the following rights with regard to your personal data:

  • Access to whether and which data we process about you (Article 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete data (Article 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure of your data (Article 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Article 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability in a common, machine-readable format (Article 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing based on a legitimate interest (Article 21 GDPR)

You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. For all of the above, an informal message to kontakt@pivon.ai is sufficient.

8. Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

If you believe that the processing of your data violates the General Data Protection Regulation, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. This right exists without prejudice to other legal remedies.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as soon as the website or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
