Consulting is a dialogue, not a monologue

What it’s about

We don’t see consulting as a monologue trying to sell you something, but as a dialogue in which we create something together. We want to find out where you stand and what you want to achieve. Together, we will turn your goals into reality.

The model

You know your organisation, we know the way.

Good consulting happens in dialogue. You bring your knowledge of people, processes and goals, and we bring our experience in AI. Together we create a plan that fits you.

  • You

    Openness and access

    • Openness and honesty
    • Access to the people, processes and data involved
    • Decision-makers who back the project
  • We

    Method and experience

    • A proven approach from analysis to roadmap
    • The triad of people, organisation and technology
    • An independent view of technologies and vendors
  • Together

    Clarity and direction

    • A robust roadmap with prioritised use cases
    • Measurable value instead of buzzwords
    • A basis on which your decision-makers can decide
The roadmap

From the first question to a robust roadmap.

In five steps we get from “where do we even start?” to a roadmap you can decide on. After that, we support you with individual consulting packages as far as you need.

  1. 01 Step 1

    Kickoff

    We get to know your organisation and clarify goals, scope and stakeholders.

    out goals, scope and stakeholders are clear

  2. 02 Step 2

    As-is/to-be analysis

    “How are you?” – we understand where you stand and where you want to go: mindset, skills, governance, processes, data and technology.

    out a clear picture of the starting point and goal

  3. 03 Step 3

    Use cases

    We collect and assess use cases by measurable value and technical feasibility.

    out prioritised use cases with measurable value

  4. 04 Step 4

    Roadmap

    The best use cases become a plan with sequence, effort and dependencies.

    out a robust roadmap

  5. 05 Step 5

    Decision

    A decision paper in your language.

    out a decision paper for those responsible

Before the kickoff

What makes getting started easier.

Questions in advance
  • What is your goal with AI – and how would you recognise success?
  • Where in your company do you see the biggest lever today?
  • What experience with AI already exists?
  • Who should take part in the kickoff?
  • Are there requirements, for example on data protection, IT or budget?
  • What timeframe do you have in mind?

Bring your answers to the kickoff – or send them to us beforehand.
