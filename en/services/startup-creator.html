A vision is the beginning of success

You have a vision or an idea and don’t know how to make it happen? We support founders from the first sketch to the first paying customer. We have the know-how and the experience to bring your business idea to life.

The model

You bring the idea, we build the platform.

Instead of capital, we invest knowledge and time: we develop your solution and run it for one year. In return, we share in the revenue.

  • You

    Vision and commitment

    • A vision or an idea for a software platform
    • Willingness to play an active part in the implementation
    • Knowing what is to be built
    • A registration fee of €1,000 as a binding commitment
    • The platform’s operating costs
  • We

    Development and operation

    • Investment of the development costs
    • Development of the solution
    • Operation of the platform for twelve months
    • Provision of the necessary infrastructure
    • Experience in architecture, AI and product development
  • Together

    Revenue share and decision

    • A share of revenue of 20–40 %, depending on effort and potential
    • After one year, we decide together whether and how to continue
    • A contract that covers everything and protects both of us
    • Regular coordination and transparency throughout our collaboration
The process

From idea to a successful business model.

We develop your platform in five steps through to go-live. We then run the platform for one year and decide together about the future.

Phase 1 Development

  1. 01 Step 1

    Kickoff

    We get to know each other, want to understand your idea and clarify the formal questions about your business model. Afterwards, we decide whether we want to work together.

    out a shared understanding and the decision to work together

  2. 02 Step 2

    Concept

    We sort and structure the requirements you have formulated and prepare them for implementation. If necessary, we repeat this step.

    ↻ 1–2 iterations if needed

    out a structured concept ready for implementation

  3. 03 Step 3

    Approval

    We review the concept together one final time and approve it.

    out an approved concept

  4. 04 Step 4

    Development

    We develop the platform, and you are available for questions. You regularly see what is being built.

    out a finished, tested platform

  5. 05 Step 5

    Acceptance & go-live

    You accept the solution, we take it live.

    out the platform live in operation

Before the kickoff

What we need from you in advance.

Commitment

€1,000

The registration fee shows that you are serious. Only then do we reserve time for your project.

Questions in advance
  • What problem does your platform solve, and for whom?
  • How will the platform make money?
  • Who are your competitors, and what do you do differently?
  • What already exists – concept, prototype, first customers?
  • What role will you take on yourself after go-live?
  • What timeframe do you have in mind?

Bring your answers to the kickoff – or send them to us beforehand.
