People

Your employees decide whether AI makes it into everyday work — not the technology. We take reservations seriously and use real tasks from your organisation to show what the tools can do and where they fall short. The result is people who can put AI into perspective instead of fearing or overestimating it.

  • Fundamentals without buzzwords
  • Prompting on your own tasks
  • Dealing with errors and made-up answers
  • Data protection in everyday work

Organisation

AI changes workflows, responsibilities and decision paths. Clarify this only after the first success and you lose time. We clarify early on who may do what, where the data comes from and how an experiment becomes a reliable process.

  • Guardrails and usage policies
  • Roles and responsibilities
  • Selecting the first use cases
  • Metrics that show you success

Technology

The toolbox is large and changes quickly. We show you which building blocks work for your case and set up an environment in which you can experiment safely.

  • Overview of models and providers
  • Privacy-compliant setup
  • First connection to your existing tools
  • Keeping an eye on operation and cost
