For us, development means solving problems

What it’s about

Requirements for software and process automation come in many different forms: a prototype, an MVP or a full solution. Our expertise allows us to meet all of your needs. We choose the technology to fit your problem, not our habits.

The model

You know the problem, we build the solution

We develop AI solutions that integrate into your day-to-day business and work reliably. Whether we write the code ourselves or use existing automation solutions, we decide together based on the requirements.

  • You

    Requirements and access

    • Business requirements and dedicated contacts
    • Access to the systems and data involved
    • Time for coordination and acceptance
  • We

    Architecture and development

    • Architecture, development and integration from a single source
    • Technology that fits your problem, not our habits
    • Operation and handover, if you wish
  • Together

    Reliability and freedom

    • A solution your business can rely on every day
    • Transparency on effort and cost at every step
    • No dependency on a single vendor
The process

From idea to operation

We take your idea into operation in five steps. But it doesn’t always take all five – sometimes a single one is enough.

  1. 01 Step 1

    Kickoff

    We clarify goals, requirements and constraints and get to know your systems.

    out goals, requirements and scope are clear

  2. 02 Step 2

    Prototype & feasibility

    Within weeks, something that actually runs answers the key questions: Is the data sufficient? Is the quality good enough? Does operation pay off?

    out a working prototype and a robust estimate of effort

  3. 03 Step 3

    Development

    We build the solution to fit into your day-to-day work instead of rebuilding it.

    out a finished, tested application

  4. 04 Step 4

    Integration

    We connect what needs connecting: systems, data sources, permissions and roles.

    out the solution works inside your systems

  5. 05 Step 5

    Operation & handover

Before the kickoff

What makes getting started easier

Questions in advance
  • What problem should the solution solve, and for whom?
  • Which systems and data does it need to work with?
  • Is there already a prototype or a concept?
  • What requirements are there for data protection, security and operation?
  • Who will run the solution after handover?
  • What time and budget frame do you have in mind?

Bring your answers to the kickoff – or send them to us beforehand.
