Prototype and feasibility

Before budget flows into a solution, we use something that actually runs to find out whether it holds up. Within weeks, a prototype answers what concept papers leave open: Is the data sufficient? Is the quality good enough? Does operation pay off?

  • Technical feasibility for your specific case
  • A robust estimate of effort and cost
  • A basis for decisions instead of gut feeling

AI applications

We develop applications that fit into your day-to-day work instead of rebuilding it.

  • Assistants and agents for recurring tasks
  • Retrieval augmented generation across your own documents
  • Analysis of unstructured data
  • Automation of process steps

Integration

A solution that sits next to your systems instead of inside them won’t get used. We connect what needs connecting — and make sure you stay free to switch.

  • Connection to existing systems and data sources
  • Interfaces and data flows
  • Permissions and role concept
  • No dependency on a single vendor

Operation and handover

For us, handover means that you can run the solution yourself. And that we can still be reached.

  • Monitoring and quality assurance
  • Cost control during operation
  • Documentation and knowledge transfer
  • Support as needed, not by contract term
