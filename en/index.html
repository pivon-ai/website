02 — Approach

Five clear steps to your goal.

Experience has taught us that methods have to fit the people and their needs. Nor should you cling dogmatically to a procedure once you notice that the circumstances have changed. That is why we reflect regularly and adapt the way we work, even while a project is under way. Even so, our five steps to the goal have proven themselves. Success needs structure – just not a rigid one.

  1. Step

    Contact

    Everything starts with getting in touch! Contact us – simply, quickly and without obligation – for an initial call.

    kontakt@pivon.ai
    +49 151 99 99 788

  2. Step

    Clarifying the brief

    Clarifying the brief sounds formal. For us, it above all means understanding in dialogue what you need – or what you might need. For us, this step is complete once you confirm that we have understood you correctly.

  3. Step

    Concept

    Once your needs are clear, we create a content concept and a robust roadmap. You know the path to the solution, how much effort it will take, what it will cost and how you can contribute yourself.

  4. Step

    Implementation

    We see implementation as the point or phase in which we bring your needs and requirements to life. It is a time of intensive collaboration and learning together.

  5. Step

    Completion

    For us, completion means making sure that you can either apply and implement our work yourself or use it in operation. And we are still there for you at any time!

03 — Our principles

People, organisation and technology in harmony.

Everything we do is based on looking at people, organisation and technology as a whole. That is the only way to create acceptance for change. Our principles are the result of successes and failures alike. They are our compass for guiding you safely through the challenges of our time.

  • People

    Responsibility stays with people.

    Technologies are tools that support people and will never replace them. We see technology as an opportunity and a chance for change. People remain at the centre of decisions.

  • Organisation

    Organisations carry the change.

    Organisations have to change and adapt as times change. Compliance, governance and skills have to evolve and create a safe framework.

  • Technology

    Measurable value instead of complexity.

    Technologies have to create measurable value without adding unnecessary complexity. Nor may they lead to a dependency that leaves you unable to act.

  • people-centred
  • transparent
  • flexible
  • technology-agnostic
  • practical
  • sustainable
  • future-oriented
  • innovative
  • responsible
